# Red Team Assessment

## What is the Problem?

Most organizations rely heavily on **perimeter defenses and compliance checklists**, yet real-world attackers do not follow rules, scope documents, or assumptions.

As a result:
- Security controls **appear compliant but fail under real attack**
- Detection and response capabilities are **rarely tested**
- Blue teams may never experience **live adversarial behavior**

This creates a **false sense of security**, especially in highly regulated sectors like banking and fintech.

---

## What I Offer

I provide **learning-focused Red Team assessments** that simulate **real attacker behavior**, not just vulnerability scans.

My approach focuses on:
- **Understanding how an attacker thinks**
- **Testing real-world exploit paths**
- **Helping teams learn, not blame**

> 💡 *I am happy to support assessments for learning and improvement — even free for early-stage or educational environments.*

---

## Assessment Scope

Depending on the environment, Red Team activities may include:

- External & Internal Network Attack Simulation
- Active Directory Enumeration & Privilege Escalation
- Credential Attacks (Password Spraying, Kerberoasting – safe & approved)
- Firewall & Segmentation Bypass Validation
- VPN, Remote Access & Cloud Entry Point Testing
- Web, API & Application Chaining Attacks
- Detection & Response (SOC / SIEM) Validation

All activities are **authorized, scoped, and documented**.

---

## Methodology

My Red Team methodology follows a **controlled adversary simulation model**:

1. **Reconnaissance**
   - Open-source intelligence (OSINT)
   - Network and service discovery

2. **Initial Access**
   - Exploitation of exposed services
   - Weak authentication paths

3. **Lateral Movement**
   - Internal trust abuse
   - Credential reuse

4. **Privilege Escalation**
   - Misconfigurations
   - Weak access controls

5. **Impact Demonstration**
   - Proof-of-access (no data damage)
   - Business risk mapping
