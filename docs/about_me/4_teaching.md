# Teaching

Teaching is an essential part of my learning journey.  
I believe that **the best way to understand a concept deeply is to explain it clearly to others**.

My teaching focuses on **simplifying complex cybersecurity and networking topics** into practical, understandable knowledge.

---

## Learning by Teaching

I actively share what I learn through:
- Written notes and documentation
- Tutorials and walkthroughs
- Real-world examples from security operations and audits  

Teaching helps me:
- Strengthen fundamentals  
- Identify gaps in understanding  
- Improve clarity and structured thinking  

---

## Topics I Teach & Share

My teaching and content primarily cover:

- Network security fundamentals  
- Firewall concepts and secure network design  
- Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing (VAPT) basics  
- Red Team concepts and attack methodologies  
- GRC, audits, and compliance mapping  
- Banking, fintech, and enterprise security practices  

All content is created with a **beginner-friendly mindset**, while remaining technically accurate.

---

## Platforms & Mediums

I share knowledge through multiple platforms, including:
- GitBook for structured documentation and notes  
- Blogs (Medium) for simplified explanations and reflections  
- Videos and walkthroughs where visual explanation adds value  

My goal is to make **security knowledge accessible**, especially for learners entering the field.

---

## Mentorship & Community Support

I am always open to:
- Helping beginners understand core concepts  
- Reviewing learning paths and study plans  
- Discussing real-world security scenarios  

> *If I can help you secure something while learning myself, it’s a win for both of us.*

---

## Teaching Philosophy

- Start with fundamentals  
- Explain the “why” before the “how”  
- Learn from mistakes and failures  
- Keep security practical and realistic  

This teaching section will continue to grow as my experience and contributions expand.
